Summary

Where Both Plans Stand

Two sequences run back to back. The MVP closes on a client demo; Phase 1 closes on a production go-live. Each has its own milestone set, feature timeline and dependency list below.

Dev Complete Is 8 September Part 1

Six sprints of feature work, then a five-stage release sequence: schema, QA, UAT, Staging, freeze. Everything before Staging runs on synthetic data.

Staging Is The Only Real-Data Pass Risk

Five days, 25 to 30 September. Every data-shaped failure, wrong totals, missing records, slow queries at volume, can only surface inside that window.

Phase 1 Ends 17 November Part 2

The feature build closes at Sprint 11. Sprint 12 is reserved for stakeholder feedback, then four weeks of QA, UAT and Staging before the 12 January go-live.

Two Items Sit Outside The Team Blocking

No date for the QA, UAT and Staging environments, and no dedicated test resource. Both land on the same dates.

Assumptions · Risks · Dependencies

Watch This

Two things sit outside the team's control and both land on the same dates: there is no date yet for the QA, UAT and Staging environments, and there is no dedicated test resource. The 22 Sep UAT start and the 5 Oct Staging demo depend on the first; the defect risk through the 25 to 30 Sep Staging window depends on the second. If either slips, the compression lands on Staging, the one window that cannot be shortened without shipping untested data behaviour into the demo.

Recommendation

Protect The Staging Window

One window in the MVP plan carries all of the real-data risk, and two unresolved items feed straight into it.

Keep 25 To 30 September Intact

Staging is the only pass on production data before the demo. Every other window in the plan can absorb a slip; this one cannot be shortened without shipping untested data behaviour into the demo.

Environments

Get a date for QA, UAT and Staging. UAT on 22 Sep and the demo on 5 Oct both depend on it.

Test Resource

A dedicated tester before the deep pass on 17 Sep, or the defect risk lands inside the Staging window.

Data Snapshot

A client data snapshot in QA around 17 Sep, so data-shaped defects surface a week early.

The Ask

Confirm Three Dates This Week

Environment readiness, the test resource start date, and the snapshot cut. All three feed the same window, and each one is owned outside the delivery team.
